About Our School

Welcome to Mandele, a school committed to providing quality education and creating a supportive environment where every learner can develop academically, socially, and personally.

Our school believes that education goes beyond the classroom. We aim to equip students with knowledge, skills, confidence, discipline, and good values that will help them become responsible members of society.

Our History

Mandele was established in 2015 with the aim of providing quality education to learners in our community. Since its establishment, the school has continued to grow from nursery school and primary school which built the secondary school section.Mandele has all the section of the basic education in Malawi.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in education, developing knowledgeable, responsible, creative, and confident learners who can make a positive difference in society.

Our Mission

To provide quality and inclusive education in a safe and supportive environment while promoting academic excellence, discipline, creativity, leadership, and good character.

Our Core Values

  • Excellence
  • Integrity
  • Respect
  • Discipline
  • Teamwork
  • Responsibility

Our Facilities

Our school provides facilities designed to support effective teaching and learning. These include classrooms, learning resources, computer facilities, science laboratories, sports facilities, and other resources.
